# The Regent: research for the third character

Sources: the Spire Codex export (stable and v0.111), the Slay the Spire wiki (slaythespire.wiki.gg pages for
Sovereign Blade, Pillar of Creation, Archaic Tooth, Meteor Shower, Falling Star), and community guides
(untapped.gg, games.gg, slaythespire2.gg, sts2front.com). Data wins over guides where they disagree.

## Character

- 75 HP, 99 Gold, 3 Energy. Unlocked in the original by winning with the Silent.
- Starting deck: 4 Strike, 4 Defend, Falling Star, Venerate (10 cards).
- Starting relic: Divine Right (start of each combat, gain 3 Stars).
- Ancient versions: Touch of Orobas turns Divine Right into Divine Destiny (7 Stars in v0.111, 6 on stable);
  Archaic Tooth turns Falling Star into Meteor Shower (upgrade and enchantment carry over).
- Ancient cards (Dusty Tome): Meteor Shower, The Sealed Throne.

## Stars

- A second resource beside Energy. Starts each combat at 0 plus Divine Right's 3.
- Stars carry over between turns and have no cap. They do not regenerate on their own.
- Cards with a Star cost need both their Energy and their Stars to be played. Stardust costs X Stars.
- Spending and gaining Stars are both events (Black Hole reacts to either; Child of the Stars, Galactic Dust and
  Mini Regent react to spending; Radiate counts Stars gained this turn).

## Forge and Sovereign Blade

- Sovereign Blade: colorless token Attack, cost 2 (1 upgraded), Retain, deals 10 damage plus everything Forged.
- The first Forge each combat creates a Sovereign Blade in hand with 10 + the Forge amount.
- Every Forge adds its amount to all existing Blades in every pile, the Exhaust pile included.
- If the Blade has left the deck (Exhausted or Transformed), the next Forge creates a new Blade at 10 + that Forge.
- Seeking Edge (hits ALL enemies), Sword Sage (Replay 1) and Conqueror (that enemy takes double damage from all
  Blades this turn) apply to every Blade, including Blades created later. Parry: the Blade also gains 10 (14)
  Block, shown on the card and affected by Dexterity and Frail; Sword Sage doubles it through Replay.
- Summon Forth puts every Blade from every pile into the hand, then Forges.
- Creating the Blade counts as creating a card.

## Creating cards

Counts: any card added to hand, draw pile or discard pile during combat (random colorless cards, Minions, copies,
the first Sovereign Blade) and Transforms (BEGONE!, CHARGE!!, GUARDS!!!). Status cards do not count (patch V0.40.0).
Used by Supermassive, Pillar of Creation (2 (3) Block each, v0.111), Arsenal (1 Strength each), Regalite
(first creation each turn, 4 Block, v0.111).

## Tokens and statuses

- Minion Strike: 0, Exhaust, deal 6 (9), draw 1. Tags Strike, Minion.
- Minion Dive Bomb: 0, Exhaust, deal 13 (16). Tag Minion.
- Minion Sacrifice: 0, Exhaust, Skill, gain 7 (10) Block. Tag Minion.
- Debris: Status, cost 1, Exhaust, no effect (Collision Course adds one; Crash Landing fills the hand).
- Vitruvian Minion (shop relic): cards containing "Minion" deal double damage and gain double Block.

## Cards (v0.111): 91, of which 5 co-op only (Constellation, Largesse, Plot, Hammer Time, Tutor)

- Basic 4, Common 20, Uncommon 38, Rare 27, Ancient 2. 22 cards cost Stars.
- New engine needs, beyond Stars and Forge:
  - When drawn: Kingly Kick (cost -1 each draw), Kingly Punch (+4 damage this combat each draw).
  - From the Exhaust pile: Bombardment plays itself at the start of your turn.
  - From the top of the draw pile: I Am Invincible plays itself at the end of your turn.
  - Returns: Make It So (into hand every 3 Skills in a turn), Particle Wall (back to hand), Shining Strike
    (onto the draw pile).
  - Counters: Skills played this turn (Lunar Blast), cards created this combat (Supermassive), Stars gained this
    turn (Radiate), hits on an enemy this turn (Beat into Shape), Energy spent (Orbit, every 4 gives 1).
  - Other: Reflect (blocked attack damage reflected this turn), Void Form (ends your turn; first 2 cards each
    turn free), Decisions, Decisions (play a chosen Skill 3 times), Heavenly Drill (X doubled at 4+),
    Foregone Conclusion, Tyranny, Monologue, Monarch's Gaze, Royalties (Gold after combat), Heirloom Hammer.
- Card text vs power text: Conqueror and Tyranny have power descriptions that disagree with the card text
  (2 turns; 2 cards). The card text and the wiki agree, so the card text is used.

## Relics and potions (v0.111)

Relics: Divine Right, Divine Destiny, Fencing Manual (Forge 10 at combat start), Galactic Dust (every 10 Stars
spent, 10 Block), Lunar Pastry (end of turn, 1 Star), Mini Regent (first Star spent each turn, 1 Strength),
Orange Dough (2 random colorless cards at combat start), Regalite, Vitruvian Minion.
Potions: Star Potion (3 Stars), King's Courage (Forge 15), Cosmic Concoction (3 upgraded colorless cards).

## Stable vs v0.111

v0.111 changes 11 cards: Alignment and Resonance (3 to 2 Stars), Guiding Star (1 Star, draw moved to next turn),
Collision Course 11 to 10, Crush Under 7 to 8, Devastate 30 to 35, Pillar of Creation (3 to 2 Block),
Refine Blade Forge 9 to 8, Spoils of Battle Forge 5 to 6, Terraforming 6 (+2) to 7 (+3), Beat into Shape text.
Relics: Divine Destiny 6 to 7, Regalite reworked. The 3 cards found only in v0.111 are co-op.
