# HallowDeep

**A deckbuilding roguelike you can play in your browser.**

### [▶ Play now](https://hallowdeep.netlify.app/)

<img src="docs/screenshots/fight.jpg" alt="A fight: the Wirebound faces three Choir monsters with a hand of five cards" width="720">

</div>

Pick one of five heroes, build a deck as you go, and fight your way up three acts. HallowDeep follows the rules and
numbers of Slay the Spire 2 (v0.111) with its own names, card art and interface. It runs on desktops, tablets and
phones, and saves as you play.

**Coming soon:** co-op for 2 to 4 players.

## Run it locally

```bash
python3 build.py
```

Then open `dist/hallowdeep.html` in a browser. Developer notes are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
